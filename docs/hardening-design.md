## Ordinary ballot eligibility

The numerical deciding VP field alone does not establish ballot eligibility. A positive minimum of at least fourteen days implies that an eligible neuron cannot be inactive: inactivity requires dissolution at least two weeks ago. Dissolving timestamps use the actual observation time; malformed dissolve state cannot become a zero observation.

`rs/nns/governance/src/network_economics.rs` defines an absent whole voting-power-economics record as `VotingPowerEconomics::DEFAULT`, including six months (each month 2,629,800 seconds). These are different optional cases. Explicit values must satisfy the reviewed fourteen-day through six-month bounds. Optional-default resolution additionally requires the exact reviewed Governance raw or gzip module hash. Unknown modules with unresolved optional defaults stop publication. This is compatibility gating, not a new deployment identity claim or a Dendrite policy change.

A round stores the entire returned voting-power-economics record, its resolved threshold, resolution provenance and Governance module hash. Every support batch brackets its same-response VP/configuration observation with context reads; selection publication and following dispatch recheck that context. A change prevents publication and requires a complete new round. This detects observed changes; it does not claim an atomic snapshot or prove no transient change between calls. Public scan APIs expose the context and interval. Missing evidence is an error, not zero support.

## Bounded scheduling and observation consistency

Production retains ten-second fallback timers and adds bounded continuations after one second. A burst allows at most sixteen transitions, a conservative shared 48-call budget, 250 million call-context instructions and ten billion cycles before yielding. The next individual bounded transition can exceed a soft burst budget; measurements report actual per-transition and checkpoint maxima. A two-trillion-cycle reserve gates dispatch. Index pages remain separately checkpointed. Leases and generations reject stale callback tails, including continuation scheduling.

`list_neurons` provides public configuration and its Governance observation timestamp, but the pinned multi-query deliberately omits known-neuron metadata from both returned structures. Collection pairs each batch of at most fifty configurations with a concurrent validated `list_known_neurons` registry read (two concurrent calls). It records the request-start and response-end interval as well as the actual configuration timestamp; all must fit within 300 seconds. Absence from a complete validated registry is confirmed absence; a failed or oversized registry read remains unresolved. Collection shares these observations and controller evidence across a round for at most 300 seconds, without per-dependency metadata timer slots. Candidate evidence spans at most 300 seconds, using the oldest actual observation as its age. Controller evidence retains both request-start and response-end bounds; report construction rechecks the oldest observation after awaits, so a slow controller response cannot silently exceed that interval. Selected validation clears the discovery cache and must complete, with cycle checks, within 1,800 seconds before publication/dispatch. Destination drafts fetch fifty recipients per batch and retain the same 1,800-second limit. A conclusively NonCompliant partial report from the unchanged evaluator skips unnecessary dependency/controller reads; an unresolved contender still blocks completion.

Pinned Dendrite requires all committed delegates to be among five to fifteen managers. A compliant candidate cannot legitimately have thirty distinct committed delegates outside its manager set. Capacity fixtures therefore use valid manager sets; a separate invalid large dependency graph must be rejected without manufacturing compliance. Shared controller/default evidence is reused; selected manager sets remain disjoint. Capacity claims must cite executed dense fixtures, not empty-score index scans.

State remains versioned snapshot-cell storage with bounded round/cache/history sizes. Checkpoint counters and encoding/stable-write instruction measurements are public. Denied setters reject the actual caller before entering mutation/serialization; internal authorization remains. Measurements decide whether snapshot storage meets the tested workload; no universal mainnet capacity claim follows.

## Historical following reconciliation

Support interval, oldest candidate validation, first intended write, observed readback and reconciliation completion are distinct fields. Reward validity ends at the earlier underlying observation deadline, never readback time. Historical writes can be reconciled after outages without permitting stale rewards. A stale or old-revision recovered roster schedules a new complete round promptly.

A fresh roster wakes blocked or in-flight payout admission and retires its old draft. It preserves the ordinary daily waiting period after a completed payout; a roster/configuration change cannot accelerate another distribution of later deposits.

Selected revalidation ends the round immediately when the unchanged evaluator establishes NonCompliant status. Indeterminate evidence continues to block and retry; it cannot silently remove an unresolved contender. A still-compliant but materially changed manager/topic set also requires a complete new round.

An already-dispatched intent survives a setter for readback. Before any new write or retry, the worker reads current mode/revision, checks observation age after awaits and uses one guarded commit checking worker generation, current revision and exact intent attempt. A mismatching old-revision readback cannot retry the obsolete roster.

## Financial obligations and attempts

An immutable obligation contains the recipient binding and equal net amount. Prepared obligations have no transfer attempt and no dispatch timestamp. First dispatch persists an attempt containing fee, deterministic memo, timestamp and execution-uncertainty flag before the Ledger await. Unknown execution preserves this exact identity. A candidate block is independently checked against authoritative Ledger/archive contents before confirmation. Missing history never permits a fresh identity for an unknown attempt.

A first-dispatch typed BadFee, InsufficientFunds, CreatedInFuture or BadBurn refusal is a definite no-execution rejection under the pinned Ledger implementation. It may permit another bounded attempt, after fresh fee/funding checks. Such an error on a retry does not erase earlier ambiguity. TooOld, transport errors, incompatible replies and uncertain attempts remain unresolved. The recent exact rejection history is bounded as documented below. Confirmed obligations are never redistributed or resent.

The initial fee and equal net entitlements remain fixed. Later fee quotes affect only unattempted or definitively rejected attempts. Additional fee funding is explicitly reserved from available balance; insufficient funding leaves the plan waiting. Already-executed attempt fees remain fixed. Dust remains reserved and incoming deposits cannot enlarge entitlements. Total confirmed cost plus reserved liabilities and dust cannot exceed the original funding plus explicitly reserved extra fees.

RC2 introduced stable V2; V3 retains its conversion of the exact V1 schema, including its original single-variant envelope when checking lossless decoding. A snapshot produced by the checksum-verified historical RC1 Wasm exercises the production upgrade path twice; native tests cover every old financial status. Prepared V1 records were durably unsent and become obligations without attempts. Every legacy Held record retains its old identity as possibly executed, even when a reason string suggests a refusal; absence from history cannot prove non-execution. Other dispatched/confirmed records retain old identities and fees. Completed payments gain separate cache-maintenance work. Legacy rosters lose reward eligibility and policy migration defaults to ObserveOnly. No funded liability is deleted. New transfer/proposal variants and observation/health fields are externally visible Candid changes; clients must update their response decoders.

## Proposal and staking maintenance contracts

In pinned `governance.rs::make_proposal`, typed errors return before proposal insertion and fee charging; no fallible returned error follows insertion. The audited precreation refusal classes (3,4,5,10,11,14,16) terminate that attempt with an observable error and one-hour backoff followed by fresh preflight. Unknown classes, transport failures, incompatible success replies and success without a positive ID remain durable ambiguous. A positive success ID receives prompt exact readback; an absent or unbound record returns to exact-history investigation without authorizing resubmission. A submitted proposal voted down is a separate status. At most one unresolved operation remains.

Governance canonicalizes management-proposal titles. Recovery compares that canonical title while binding the exact summary marker, URL, proposer, target, 100% DisburseMaturity action and explicit canister default destination. A title marker alone never supplies idempotence.

The owned proposer is bound to this canister's controller and deterministic fixed-nonce staking account before refresh. Ready/bootstrap and proposal preflight refresh by verified neuron ID before evaluating cached fee affordability. Bootstrap funding remains separate from rewards.

Pinned `manage_neuron` handles ClaimOrRefresh(ByNeuronIdOrSubaccount) before controller/hotkey authorization; it can refresh an existing public recipient without obtaining authority. Production validates the original staking subaccount, invokes that supported refresh and verifies protected identity, controller, hotkeys, visibility, following and dissolve settings afterward. The following map is compared by topic rather than wire entry order: the pinned API constructs an unordered map and may serialize unchanged entries in a different order. Cache/weighted age maintenance is distinct from payment confirmation. A refresh failure exposes durable RetryAt/Abandoned maintenance independently of financially settled payouts; upgrades never resend confirmed ICP. This does not request keys, add authority or redirect funds.

No production write, deployment, funding or push is part of qualification. Operators must review the policy/schema changes, controllers, pins, live authority, funding and actual CI evidence before separately authorizing activation.


## Final financial lifecycle contracts (V3)

Financial settlement is exactly `Plan::complete`: every obligation has an authoritative exact Ledger confirmation. Maintenance completion is separate. The payment job retires a settled plan and persists a full-day `payout_not_before`; publication, upgrades, deposits and maintenance cannot accelerate admission. The existing completed-plan bound is 32, including maintenance records. Job 4 chooses earliest due work, persists its retry before await, and validates its exact retained plan/recipient/binding before callback commit. Delayed, failed, private or missing recipients cannot monopolize it. Eight attempts with hour-to-day backoff end in explicit Abandoned status. Evicted unfinished maintenance has an aggregate counter; it is never called successful. Only settled plans enter retention; unknown transfers remain funded active liabilities indefinitely.

Conclusive first-dispatch Ledger refusals retain their exact attempt. Each twelve-attempt episode is followed by a durable one-day Cooldown, then fresh fee/balance/authority checks. There is no identity reset for Unknown, Verifying or possibly executed Held attempts. The existing rejected-attempt vector retains the twelve most recent exact conclusively unexecuted identities, in order; older such audit details are evicted, with their count apparent from the monotonic current attempt number. No hash archive or new history subsystem is introduced. This bound applies only to proven refusals, never an ambiguous/current attempt or confirmed payment. Previous retained exact refusals remain distinct from possibly executed attempts. Net entitlements, fee reservations and plan/recipient identities never change across episodes. V2 exhausted Held states convert only with a persisted `may_have_executed=false` proof, independently of reason text; legacy ambiguity remains protected.

The pinned Governance proof for missed maturity windows is unusually important. In `rs/nns/governance/src/governance/disburse_maturity.rs::try_finalize_maturity_disbursement`, finalization takes the neuron lock, pops progress, then awaits `MintIcpOperation`. Mint success releases the lock; failure restores progress before unlocking, or retains the lock if restoration fails. Thus a temporarily empty progress list does not prove completion. `governance.rs::refresh_neuron` acquires the same lock before its Ledger balance await. Its exact successful ClaimOrRefresh ID, or its typed InsufficientFunds (14) after that lock/balance read, supplies a barrier. An empty-before/barrier/empty-after sequence with protected configuration unchanged and a 300-second observation bound, plus retained exact Executed proof, establishes operation termination. A failed barrier or unavailable/contradictory progress never does. Ordinary preflight still gates the next distinct proposal.
